Help

What is a pixel?

Pixels determine the width and height of your picture. A 192 × 192 image is 192 tiny dots wide and 192 tall.

What is KB and MB?

They measure file size. 1 MB = 1024 KB. A phone photo is often 2–5 MB; many forms ask for 20–200 KB.

Why does an image become blurry after compression?

JPEG and WebP shrink files by throwing away fine detail. Lower quality or smaller pixel size means a smaller file but a softer picture.

Why can a PDF sometimes become larger after processing?

If a PDF is already optimized, redrawing its pages as pictures can add size. Smriti EduSaar then keeps your original and tells you.

Why might the exact requested file size not be possible?

File size depends on picture content. We can get under a limit, but landing on exactly 100.0 KB is not guaranteed. Very small limits may not be reachable at readable quality.
